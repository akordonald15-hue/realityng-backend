# RealityNG Moderation, Fraud and Incident Operations

Status: Operational baseline; legal reporting duties and response targets **REQUIRE PROFESSIONAL REVIEW**.

| Procedure | Primary owner | Initial target | Evidence | Escalation / closure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fraud or suspicious listing/provider | Trust & Safety | Acknowledge within 1 business day | URLs, IDs, messages, payment references, minimal screenshots | Restrict visibility/account; escalate material loss; record decision and reporter notice |
| Identity/verification abuse | Verification Operations | Same business day for active impersonation | Submission IDs, identity mismatch, access logs | Suspend signals/account; privacy/security escalation; documented reinstatement or closure |
| Financing abuse | Financial Operations | Same business day | Application/consent/submission audit IDs | Disable submission, notify approved partner, legal/privacy escalation |
| Escrow abuse | Financial Operations | Immediate for active funds risk | Escrow/provider/event/reconciliation IDs | Freeze platform instructions, contact provider, incident lead; never promise recovery |
| Harassment/messaging abuse | Trust & Safety | Same day for credible threats | Message/thread IDs; preserve controlled copy | Restrict contact/account; emergency services where appropriate; appeal path |
| Moderation appeal | Independent moderation reviewer | 5 business days | Original decision, policy rule, new evidence | Uphold/reverse; reason and audit record |
| Security incident | Incident commander | Immediate triage | Logs, affected systems/data, timeline | Containment, evidence preservation, counsel/privacy assessment, post-incident report |
| Privacy incident | Privacy lead | Immediate triage | Data classes, subjects, recipients, exposure window | Contain; professional notification assessment; subject/support closure |
| Financial dispute | Financial Operations | 1 business day | Transaction, provider, consent, event and communication records | Provider/contract path; legal escalation; record outcome |
| Emergency escalation | On-call incident lead | Immediate | Minimum necessary verified facts | Leadership/counsel/provider/emergency authority as appropriate |

Case access is need-to-know. Staff must avoid exporting unnecessary private documents. Evidence preservation must be documented and time-limited. Users receive status and outcome information unless doing so would expose another person, compromise security, or violate an approved restriction.
