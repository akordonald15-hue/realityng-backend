## Required policy rules

- Terms and Privacy acceptance must be explicit, separately recorded, versioned, timestamped, and attributable.
- Marketing, non-essential analytics, and non-essential cookies must not be bundled with account creation. No evidence of production marketing consent or cookie preference storage was found.
- Verification, inspection, construction, listing, escrow, and financing signals are limited workflow records, not guarantees or professional advice.
- Private documents require least-privilege access and non-public storage. Sprint 15 evidence confirms authorization protections; retention and erasure policy remains subject to approval.
- Refund/cancellation language must identify the contracting party and must not imply RealityNG custody or a guaranteed remedy.
- Incident and complaint channels must not solicit secrets, passwords, full payment credentials, or unnecessary identity documents.

## Current public wording findings

Existing Terms, Privacy, deletion, safety, verification, listing and refund pages are helpful but labeled as drafts and contain stale future-roadmap/payment language after v2.6.0. They require approved replacement wording. Registration previously lacked explicit acceptance; Sprint 16 adds versioned proof. Dedicated escrow, financing, fraud-reporting and consent-withdrawal explanations were incomplete.

## Approval rule

Drafts may support internal QA only. Publication for beta reliance, policy-version changes, and activation of partners are **REQUIRES PROFESSIONAL REVIEW** decisions.
